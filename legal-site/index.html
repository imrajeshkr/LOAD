LOAD

Personal training, computed from your actual history.

LOAD is a strength training coach for your phone. It builds your program from your goals, experience, and equipment, adjusts it as you log real sessions, and answers questions with an AI coach that actually knows your training history — not a generic chatbot.

What it does

  • A real plan, built from your goal, experience, training days, and equipment — not a template.
  • Fast logging — pre-filled sets, one tap to confirm, built for use between sets, not after the gym.
  • A coach that knows you — ask it anything, or log a session in plain language, and it reasons from your real data.
  • Progress that's actually tracked — every session compared to your own last time, not a static target.
Available on Android and iOS. LOAD is under active development by a solo developer — questions, feedback, and bug reports are always welcome at the email below.

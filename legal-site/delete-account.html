Delete Your Account

Last updated: August 12, 2026

You can request deletion of your LOAD account and all associated data at any time. This is separate from uninstalling the app — uninstalling alone does not delete your data.

How to request deletion

Email kr.rajesh117@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject "Delete my LOAD account." We process deletion requests by hand, promptly — typically within a few days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your training profile (goal, experience, injuries/constraints, environment, split preference)
  • All logged workouts, sets, and session history
  • Bodyweight and protein entries
  • Your coach chat history

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once processed, no further copies of your data are retained by us beyond what may briefly persist in routine infrastructure backups, which are themselves purged on a normal rotation shortly after.

Partial requests

If you'd rather correct specific data, or delete only certain information rather than your whole account, say so in your email and we'll handle it accordingly — see the Privacy Policy for the full list of your rights.

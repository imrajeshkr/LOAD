Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

LOAD ("LOAD," "we," "us," or "our") is a personal strength-training app that builds a training plan around your real history and lets you log workouts and talk to an AI coach about them. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the LOAD mobile app on iOS and Android.

We are a small, independently operated app. If anything here is unclear, email kr.rajesh117@gmail.com and a real person (the developer) will answer.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address, and a securely hashed password if you sign up with email — we never see or store your password in plain text.
  • If you use "Continue with Google," your name and email address as provided by Google.

Training profile

  • Goal, experience level, training environment, days per week, and split preference, as you enter them during setup.
  • Injuries, physical constraints, or limitations you choose to tell us about — this exists specifically so the app can avoid or flag movements that could aggravate them.

Body and training data

  • Bodyweight entries you log.
  • Workout sessions: exercises, sets, reps, weight used, dates and times, and any notes, RPE, or pain you record after a session.
  • Protein intake you log.

Coach conversations

  • Messages you send to the in-app AI coach, and the coach's replies. To generate a reply, relevant parts of your training profile and recent history are included alongside your message — see "Third parties," below.

Automatically derived data

  • Things we compute from the above so the app can show them back to you: streaks, trends, progress comparisons, and similar summaries. We don't collect anything new to produce these.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers beyond what's strictly needed for the app to function and for standard crash/error diagnostics.

2. How we use your information

  • To build and adjust your training plan.
  • To let you log workouts, weight, and protein, and to show your history and progress back to you.
  • To power the AI coach's answers with your real context, rather than generic advice.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the app itself (bug fixing, reliability).

We do not use your data to serve ads, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone, for any purpose.

3. Third parties we share data with

We use a small number of service providers to run LOAD. We don't sell your data to anyone; the sharing below exists only to make the app work.

  • Supabase — our database, authentication, and file-storage provider. All of your account and training data is stored on Supabase's infrastructure, access-controlled so that only your own signed-in account can read or write your own rows.
  • Google (Gemini API) — when you message the AI coach, your message and the relevant training context needed to answer it are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply. Google's own privacy terms govern its processing of that request. We do not send Gemini any more of your data than a given reply actually needs.
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your basic profile (name, email) with us to create your account.

We do not share your data with data brokers, advertisers, or any party for marketing purposes.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active, so your training history stays intact and useful. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time — see Delete Your Account for how. We're a small team without a fully self-serve deletion flow yet — we process these requests by hand, promptly, rather than making you wait on a feature we haven't shipped.

5. Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you can ask us at any time to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data (see §4).
  • Withdraw consent to any processing that relies on it.

Email kr.rajesh117@gmail.com to exercise any of these.

If you're a Washington resident: some of the information LOAD collects (bodyweight, injuries, training history) may qualify as "consumer health data" under Washington's My Health My Data Act. In addition to the rights in §5, you have the right to withdraw consent to our collection or sharing of this data at any time, and we do not sell consumer health data or use it for targeted advertising. To exercise these rights, use the contact above.

6. Security

Your data is protected by row-level access rules at the database level — your account can only ever read or write your own rows, not other users'. All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we design for this deliberately rather than as an afterthought.

7. Children's privacy

LOAD is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of LOAD after a change means you accept the updated policy.
